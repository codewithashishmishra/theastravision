Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 May 2026 · TheAstravision (“we”, “us”)

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TheAstravision collects and uses information when you visit theastravision.com (the “Website”) and when you use AASTRAA HRMS (the “Platform”). By using our Website or Platform, you agree to this policy.

2. Who we are

Data controller: TheAstravision
Contact: ashishmishra@theastravision.com
Website: https://theastravision.com/

3. Information we collect on this Website

  • Contact / demo forms: Name, work email, company name, and message you submit.
  • Visit statistics: We use a privacy-friendly page-view counter (CountAPI) to record aggregate daily and all-time visit counts. This does not store your name or personal profile.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device type, and pages viewed may be processed by our hosting provider (e.g. Cloudflare) for security and delivery.
  • Cookies & local storage: Session storage may be used so we count one visit per browser session; chatbot and pricing preferences may use local storage on your device.

4. AASTRAA HRMS Platform data (customers)

If your employer uses AASTRAA HRMS, we process employee and HR data on behalf of your organization under their instructions, including but not limited to:

  • Employee profiles, attendance, leave, and payroll
  • Recruitment applications, resumes, and interview recordings (with consent where required)
  • WFH desktop tracker sessions, activity timelines, and policy-based screenshots (only with employee consent)
  • Expenses, helpdesk tickets, performance reviews, and audit logs

Employees should contact their employer (the data controller) for access, correction, or deletion requests regarding employment data.

5. AI features

Our Platform may use AI for resume matching, voice/video screening, attrition analytics, and helpdesk responses. Candidate interviews require explicit consent before recording. AI outputs are assistive; human reviewers make final employment decisions.

6. How we use information

  • Respond to demo and sales enquiries
  • Operate, secure, and improve the Website and Platform
  • Provide HR, payroll, attendance, recruitment, and WFH features to authorized users
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

7. Third-party services

We may share data with service providers who help us run our services, such as:

  • Cloud hosting and CDN (e.g. Cloudflare)
  • Form delivery (e.g. Web3Forms or similar email relay)
  • Aggregate visit counting (CountAPI)
  • Fonts and icons (Google Fonts, Font Awesome CDN)
  • AI inference providers (for Platform features, under contract)

We do not sell your personal information.

8. Data retention

Website enquiry data is retained as long as needed for sales follow-up. Platform data retention is configured per tenant (e.g. WFH screenshot retention days). Security and compliance audit logs are retained for at least 180 days unless a longer period is required by law or contract. Visit counters are aggregate statistics without personal identifiers.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), field-level encryption for sensitive payroll identifiers, role-based access controls, multi-factor authentication for privileged roles, and immutable-style audit logging on the Platform. Access tokens are not stored in browser local storage. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

10. Your rights (India — DPDPA and global)

If you are in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) may grant you rights including access, correction, erasure, and grievance redressal regarding personal data we process as a data processor on behalf of your employer. Contact your employer first for employment-related requests. You may also contact our grievance officer at ashishmishra@theastravision.com.

Users in the United States and other regions may have additional rights under applicable state or national privacy laws (e.g. access, deletion, opt-out of sale/sharing). We do not sell personal information. Contact us at the email above for privacy requests related to the Website.

11. Cross-border processing & subprocessors

Platform data may be processed in India and other countries where our infrastructure or subprocessors operate. Subprocessors may include cloud hosting (e.g. Cloudflare), object storage, email delivery, and AI inference providers (e.g. OpenAI) under data-processing terms. We require appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers where required by law.

12. Breach notification

We maintain incident response procedures. Where required by applicable law (including DPDPA timelines for significant personal-data breaches in India), we will notify affected customers and regulators without undue delay.

13. Children

Our services are intended for businesses and workforce users. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

14. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use of the Website after changes constitutes acceptance.
